## General findings
1. **Even splits are assumptions throughout.** For every multi-row split (okla, nin, catawba, effingham, susq, chey, hyp5), the source gives at most a start and an end state, never a year table. Each of these rows should be tagged [Assumed] for the split. For the single-amount end-date rows (pecos, rome, aragon, vizag), the date is the end state or an inferred end state. For Pecos, Chevron's 2028 first power and, for Vizag, the July 2028 commissioning target suggest the capacity starts earlier.
2. **power_approved needs a visible regulator approval.** Only NIPSCO–AWS (IURC order 46322) and Georgia Power–OpenAI (Georgia PSC, 2026-08) meet this bar. Talen–Amazon is an executed generation PPA that needs no approval, so its MW is not campus load. Black Hills–Google has no contract approval visible. OG&E–Google and Entergy–Meta are explicitly pending.
3. **Reduction and flexibility terms.** NIPSCO: one-time cut of 1,200 MW by 2029-03-31, so only the firm 1,200 is proposed, which is a sound choice. Georgia Power: up to 1,000 MW of curtailable demand response, which does not reduce the 3,200 MW of contracted load.
4. **Dedupe against t2_export.csv.** No duplicates found. AWS Berwick (cp_amzn_brw plus T1 108) is the same campus as the Talen/Salem Twp rows, and the gatherer's netting of 203 MW facility is correct. hyp5 replaces cpx_meta_hyp5 (inc=0), which is a judgment call for Andy.
